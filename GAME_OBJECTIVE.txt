LEXIA - THE KINGDOM OF SILENCE
An isekai game to become very good at the TOEIC
==========================================================

1. THE IDEA IN ONE SENTENCE
---------------------------
A top-down 2D RPG (Zelda-like, 16-bit pixel art) where you fight with your English:
every fight is a series of TOEIC questions. The hidden goal of the game: make the player
addicted, so they keep improving until they reach a very good TOEIC score.


2. ENTERING THE GAME
--------------------
- The game opens from the TOEIC app (in its own window).
- The player needs a score first: the one from the level test of the tutorial, or a score
  typed by hand.
- Then the player chooses their new life (their goal):
    * defeat the Demon King;
    * defeat a threatening enemy (protect the village);
    * live a peaceful life;
    * or just be here and do nothing special.
- The story follows this goal.


3. THE STORY
------------
- An isekai: the player dies in our world (running to their exam) and is reborn in a world
  where words are weapons.
- An epic story, with drama: characters die.
- A long adventure, with many side quests.
- Every few nights, the Demon King attacks the starting village (or a village nearby):
  monsters come to kill the villagers.


4. FIGHTS: THE HEART OF THE GAME
--------------------------------
- You fight with your English level: you must give the right answers.
- The monster's life is a bar. A good answer removes part of it (half a heart by default).
  A wrong answer lets the monster strike.
- The faster and the more correct the answer, the sooner you strike, even if you are slow.
- The time to answer depends on the speed of the player AND of the monster:
  a much faster monster leaves much less time.
- Hits can be critical.
- The XP earned depends on the percentage of good answers in the fight.
- Monsters can drop items.
- Monsters have stats: health, attack, magic attack, resistance, magic resistance, speed.

Difficulty by monster:
- small monsters: short questions (RC 10, RC 8), easy to normal, sometimes hard;
- big monsters: hard questions;
- demons and the Demon King: the long reading texts (RC 55).


5. LEARNING (WHAT MAKES THE GAME USEFUL)
----------------------------------------
- Each monster has a TYPE = a grammar category (gerunds, tenses, prepositions...).
- The type shows on the monster (color, item...). E.g. pink slime = gerunds,
  yellow slime = tenses.
- The questions of a fight are of the monster's type.
- The game trains first what the player needs to work on. When the goal is reached in a
  category, the training switches automatically to the next one.
- Like the "recovery" of the app: what was failed (or mastered) comes back at the right time.
- The player's skills = the grammar categories. Magic is based on them too.
- Every answer given in the game counts in the app's stats.


6. THE HERO
-----------
- Levels, XP, upgradable equipment (weapons, armor...). A weapon increases the damage.
- Potions.
- Magic, which depends on their skills.
- Elements: lightning, water, fire...
- Many powers, with satisfying animations.
- Two hidden powers:
    * OBSIDIAN: create barriers, pets, soldiers and weapons made of obsidian that fight
      with the hero;
    * LIGHTNING: one-shot the monsters nearby, create a zone that increases their power,
      throw a lightning ball...
- The hero can have friends, companions (bots), pets, and recruit people to fight with them.


7. DEATH
--------
- At the beginning, the player is only knocked out: a girl of the village carries them home.
- After that, death is final: everything restarts from the beginning.


8. LIFE IN THE WORLD
--------------------
- Talk with the NPCs and discover their lives.
- NPCs form couples, have babies and families. Many interactions, like in real life.
- Helping people raises the happiness of the village, and happiness helps in interactions.
- Relationships with girls (many anime waifus).
- Play games with the NPCs.
- Negotiating with a merchant (e.g. the blacksmith) also uses the question fight system.
- Dig the ground to find items.


9. MONEY AND FARMING
--------------------
- The currency is called the LEXIS.
- Lexis are earned by working, "the old way": helping others, planting carrots, writing,
  killing monsters...
- The game is long to farm, like in real life.
- Maps are bought in shops.


10. THE WORLD AND THE MAP
-------------------------
- A big map, with zones: villages, surroundings, dungeons, treasures.
- A minimap, always available.
- The map is more or less precise depending on the map the player owns.
- A day / night cycle.
- Dungeons.
- Style of the world map (reference image): a painted island, a volcanic land with the
  demon castle, a snowy region, forests, rivers with bridges, a desert, the sea. Place names
  with icons, a legend (you, main quest, side quest, town, dungeon, shop, inn, point of
  interest, bridge, blocked path), the player's party, a compass, and a panel with the
  selected place and the quests.


11. THE LOOK (ART DIRECTION)
----------------------------
- 16-bit pixel art, very beautiful, satisfying to look at.
- Dark blue windows with a light border, menus with a cursor, a pixel font.
- HUD: hero portrait, level, HP (red) and MP (blue) bars, current objective, minimap,
  zone name.
- Fight: the hero and the monster face to face, a red health bar above the monster,
  an Attack / Defense / Item / Flee menu, the sentence to complete and 4 answers.
- Dialogs: the character's portrait, their name in yellow, a choice menu (Talk, Shop,
  Quest, Leave).
- Stats menu: each category with its level (Lv. xx / 100) and a difficulty badge
  (Easy, Normal, Difficult, Extreme).
- Raid: the village on fire, demons, villagers on the ground.
- Particles, animations. For the Demon King and the great enemies of the demon land:
  the long texts (RC 55).


12. THE CODE
------------
- Clean code, readable by a human, as simple as possible.
- One function = one feature, with clear names.


13. WHAT THE CURRENT VERSION DOES (may change, see part 2)
---------------------------------------------------------
- Answers are chosen among 4 options (the question bank is multiple choice), they are not
  typed on the keyboard.
- The fight menu is Attack / Magic / Item / Flee (Magic replaces Defense).
- Aria can carry the hero home 3 times; after that, death erases the game (not the app's stats).
- The Demon King attacks the village every 7 nights.
- The door of the demon castle stays sealed until General Barbarism is defeated
  (the "blocked path" of the map).
- Without a map, you only see what you explored; Luna's maps show the roads, then the whole
  island, then the treasures.

PART 2 - MISSING DETAILS (additions)
==========================================================
These are first proposals. Names marked (TBD) and all numbers can be changed after testing.

14. PITCH AND PLATFORM
----------------------
Pitch: "You are summoned into a fantasy world where your English is your weapon:
every TOEIC question you answer is a strike against a monster."

Platform: web browser (GitHub Pages), PC first. Mobile with touch controls later.
Target players: people preparing for the TOEIC (around 400 to 900 points), 16+.


15. CONTROLS
------------
PC keyboard:
- Arrows or WASD: move
- Enter or Space: talk / confirm
- 1-4 or A-D: choose an answer in a fight
- M: world map
- I: inventory
- Esc: pause menu
Mouse: click answers and menu buttons.
Mobile (later): on-screen joystick, tap the answers.


16. HOW EACH LIFE ENDS (WIN CONDITIONS)
---------------------------------------
- Defeat the Demon King: win the final fight in the demon castle.
- Defeat a threatening enemy: defeat the boss you chose at the end of its quest line.
- Live in peace: own a house, reach 100% village happiness, and survive 3 raids.
- Just be there: no ending. Free mode, the player stops when they want.
After an ending: an epilogue that matches the chosen life, then credits.
The player can keep playing afterwards (post-game).


17. KNOCKED OUT VS REAL DEATH
-----------------------------
- Before the first big death in the story (end of chapter 2):
  losing = knocked out. The girl carries you home. You lose 10% of your Lexis.
- After that event: death is real and restarts the game from zero.
- The game clearly warns the player when this change happens.
- Learning progress (TOEIC stats, mistakes) is NEVER lost, even after a death.


18. FIRST NUMBERS (to balance after tests)
------------------------------------------
Player:
- HP: 100 at level 1, +10 per level
- XP needed for the next level: 100 x current level

Fights:
- Good answer: 20 damage. Critical = x2 damage.
- Critical: answer in less than 1/3 of the allowed time.
- Strike first: answer in less than half of the allowed time.
- Wrong answer or time over: the monster hits you.
- Answer time = base time x (player speed / monster speed)
  Base time: 20 s for a short question (RC 10 / RC 8), 45 s per question for a text (RC 55).
- XP won = monster XP x % of good answers.

Monsters:
  Type           HP    Hit   Questions   XP    Lexis
  Small (slime)   40    10    2-4         10    5
  Medium         100    20    5-8         30    15
  Boss           300    35    10-15      150    80
  Demon (RC 55)  200    30    1 text      80    50

Prices (Lexis):
- Small potion: 20 / Big potion: 60
- Weapons: 100 to 500
- Region map: 150


19. THE HERO
------------
- Name chosen at the start.
- Pixel avatar: 2 bodies, 4 hair styles, 4 hair colors, 4 outfit colors.
- No class at the start: your best grammar types decide your magic.
  Example: strong in tenses = access to fire magic first.


20. STORY (CHAPTERS)
--------------------
Prologue - The summoning
  The level test is the summoning ritual. You wake up on the island beach.
  The girl (name TBD) finds you and brings you to the village. You choose your life.

Chapter 1 - The village
  First fights against slimes. Meet the blacksmith. Buy the first map.
  First raid: you are knocked out, the girl carries you home.

Chapter 2 - Rivers and bridges
  First dungeon. You discover the hidden power Lightning.
  End of chapter: the old mentor (name TBD) dies protecting the village.
  From now on, death is real.

Chapter 3 - The snow mountains
  Harder grammar. A villager family loses someone during a raid.

Chapter 4 - The desert
  You discover the hidden power Obsidian. You prepare the final attack.

Chapter 5 - The demon castle
  Demons ask RC 55 texts. Final fight against the Demon King.

Epilogue: different for each chosen life.


21. CONTENT LIST (first version)
--------------------------------
Zones (from the world map) and their grammar type:
- Starting village: safe zone, no fights
- Forest: gerunds / infinitives (pink slimes)
- Rivers and bridges: tenses (yellow slimes)
- Snow mountains: prepositions, conditionals
- Desert: relative pronouns, word forms
- Demon castle: everything mixed + RC 55 texts

Dungeons (1 per zone):
- Slime cave (forest)
- Flooded ruins (rivers)
- Ice crypt (snow)
- Sand temple (desert)
- Castle dungeon (demon castle)

Side quests: 3 per zone at the start.
Examples: find a lost child, bring iron to the blacksmith, clean the well of slimes.

Items:
- Potions: small HP, big HP, time potion (+5 s to answer), hint potion (removes 1 wrong answer)
- Weapons: wooden sword > iron > steel > legendary
- Armor: 3 levels
- Maps: 1 per region, each makes the minimap more precise


22. INTERFACE
-------------
Main menu: New game / Continue / Options / Credits

Fight screen:
- Player HP bar and monster HP bar
- Monster grammar type icon
- Timer bar
- Question + 4 answers
- Combo counter

Exploration screen: HP, Lexis, time of day, minimap, current quest.

Pause menu (Esc): Resume / Stats / Inventory / Map / Quests / Options / Save / Quit

Options:
- Music volume, effects volume, text speed
- Relaxed mode: +50% answer time (for beginners)

End-of-fight screen: XP, Lexis, % good answers, mistakes to review.

Game over screens:
- Knocked out: "You were carried home..."
- Real death: "Your story ends here" + summary of your progress in English.


23. SOUND
---------
Music: village (calm), exploration, fight, boss, raid, night, sad theme, ending.
Effects: good answer, wrong answer, critical hit, level up, Lexis coin, menu click, footsteps.
Sources: free assets (check each license) or made with free tools (BeepBox for music,
jsfxr for effects).


24. SAVING
----------
- Automatic save in the browser (localStorage) after each fight and when entering a village.
- 3 save slots.
- Export / import the save as a file, so it is not lost when changing browser.
- After a real death, the game save of that slot is erased (learning stats are kept).


25. LINK WITH THE TOEIC APP
---------------------------
Shared between the game and the app:
- The TOEIC score
- Stats by grammar type (good and bad answers)
- Mistakes: a wrong answer in the game goes into the app's recovery list
Game only: level, Lexis, items, story progress.
Weak grammar types in the app appear more often as monsters in the game.


26. MVP AND BUILD ORDER
-----------------------
MVP (v0.1) = the smallest playable version:
- Start screen + score (level test or typed)
- Starting village with 2 NPCs
- Forest zone with pink and yellow slimes
- Fight system: questions, timer, HP, critical, XP
- Level up + Stats menu
- Save in localStorage

Then, in this order:
- v0.2  Lexis, blacksmith, shop, potions, equipment
- v0.3  World map, minimap, day/night cycle
- v0.4  First dungeon, side quests, prologue + chapter 1
- v0.5  Raids + knocked out system
- v0.6  Magic, elements, hidden powers
- v0.7  Village life: families, happiness, relationships, mini-games, digging
- v0.8  Other zones and chapters 2 to 4
- v0.9  Sound, animations, polish
- v1.0  Demon castle, endings, real death system


27. TESTS
---------
- After each version: play 15 minutes yourself and write down the bugs.
- From the MVP: 2-3 testers (ideally people preparing the TOEIC).
  Watch them play without helping. Ask: fun? too hard? too slow?
- Targets: a small fight lasts about 1 minute, a boss about 10 minutes.

PART 3 - THE ISEKAI SIDE (what the current version does)
==========================================================

28. THE WORLD
-------------
- The world is built from the painting art/MAP2.png (tools/build_world.py). New places of the painting:
  Riverford, Sailor's Refuge, the Forgotten Crypt, the Molten Mines, Murkwood Swamp, the Old Road,
  Emerald Falls, the Stormbreak Isles. A save of an older world wakes up at home (stats, gear, friends kept).
- The torch: the hero always has one. Key T (or the HUD) lights it or puts it out. It burns 5 real minutes.
  Under the sky, the rain or a strong wind (a new part of the weather) puts it out.
- Lightning is rare now (a storm is rarer, a strike every 35-80 s, it seldom aims at people).

29. THE ARMY OF THE DEMON KING
-----------------------------
- 17 new monsters from art/Monsters/Demon king (tools/cut_monsters.py): soldiers, archers, priests,
  hounds, succubi, royal guards, dark paladins, behemoths, liches, hellknights, generals.
- The Castle of Silence has 5 floors: Ravnos, Thamuz, Velmora, then Lilith the Demon Queen, then Solecis.
- Monsters look where they really walk (no more flicker when going right), walk in 4 steps, breathe when
  standing, raise dust when they run.

30. THE ISEKAI STORY AND THINGS (logic/isekai/, data/isekai.js)
---------------------------------------------------------------
- The Lost Hero: Solecis was the first summoned hero (Louis). 6 graves hide the pages of his diary.
  With all of them, the hero knows his name: the king loses 25% HP in the last fight, the ending tells more.
- The classmates: Lea (summoned by Queen Sera, a rival: a duel of words each day) and Hugo (reborn as a
  baby, innkeeper of Riverford, sells curry, ramen and mayonnaise). "Wait... I know you!" once with each.
- Curses: some monsters drop cursed accessories (strong, but stuck, and they drink HP). A rite of words
  at a goddess shrine or a church lifts the curse.
- Hot springs (5): a bath heals everything; the friends of the party bathe too.
- Arenas (in each capital): 3 duels of words in a row, once a day, for Lexis and the title of Champion.
- Events of the world (some mornings): Blood Moon, Monster Stampede (emergency quest of the Guild),
  Night of Falling Stars (Star Shards to pick up).
- 5 new classes: Sword Saint (Lea), Otherworld Merchant (Hugo), Sage (Yuki), Saint (Aria), Shadow Assassin (Nyx).
- The villagers greet the hero, chat together, meet on the square in the evening.
- Light: windows lit in the evening, torches of the knights, glow of the demons, shadows of the clouds,
  gusts of wind. The Diary tab (Quests menu) shows the event of the day, the pages and the classmates.
