LEXIA - REALISM
A world that feels alive, where every act has a consequence
==========================================================

This file adds to GAME_OBJECTIVE.txt. It lists the realistic mechanics of the game:
what is already in the game, and what to add.
All the numbers are first proposals, to balance after tests.

Time in the game: 1 day = 8 real minutes, 1 hour = 20 real seconds.
The saved state is ready in src/game/logic/realism/state.js (game.realism), with the tables in
src/game/data/realism.js and the items in src/game/data/items/realism.js. A field name in
[brackets] is the place in game.realism where the mechanic keeps its data.


==========================================================
PART 1 - THE RULES OF REALISM
==========================================================

1. WHAT MAKES A WORLD FEEL REAL
-------------------------------
Not the number of bars to watch. Three things:
- CONSEQUENCES: what the player does changes something, and it lasts.
- MEMORY: the world and the people remember what happened.
- COHERENCE: everything follows the same logic (fire burns, water puts it out, meat rots,
  a shop closes at night, a dead person stays dead).


2. THE GOLDEN RULES (every new mechanic must follow them)
--------------------------------------------------------
- English first. A realistic mechanic either creates a moment of English (a text to read,
  a question to answer), or it is handled in a few seconds. Never a long menu to manage.
- Never in a question. Nothing realistic interrupts or slows a fight question
  (no "you are hungry" popup while the player reads).
- Always warned. A danger is shown before it hurts: a sign on the HUD, a line from a
  friend, a smell, a sound. No hidden punishment.
- Always a way out. Every problem has a cure the player can find (a shop, a recipe, a person).
- Learning is never lost. TOEIC stats and mistakes are never touched by realism.
- Show it only when it matters. A new bar appears on the HUD only when it is low or high.
- One mechanic = one file in logic/realism/, with clear function names (see the code style
  in GAME_OBJECTIVE.txt, part 12).


3. ALREADY IN THE GAME (do not build it again)
---------------------------------------------
- Hunger, thirst, sleep fatigue, untreated river water (boil it), cold (survival.js)
- Weather: rain, storm, wind, lightning, seasons (weather.js, clock.js)
- Day and night, the torch that burns and goes out in the rain (torch.js)
- Weight of the bag, carry capacity, packs (weight.js)
- Wear of the equipment, repair (durability.js)
- A real economy: town treasuries, workshops, haulers, ships, scarcity prices (economy/)
- Laws, borders, contraband, prison (kingdomLaws.js)
- Languages and ancient runes (languages.js)
- Families, love, babies, children who grow up (families.js)
- Villagers who work by day and meet in the evening (villagers.js, villagerLife.js)
- Raids, wars, caravans, bandits (raid.js, war/)
- Hospital, feasts, disasters, horses, trading (society/)
- Guild ranks, contracts, wages of the fighters (guild/)
- Curses, hot springs, world events (isekai/)


==========================================================
PART 2 - THE MECHANICS TO ADD
==========================================================
Priority: (A) first, (B) then, (C) later.


4. THE BODY: WOUNDS AND HEALTH
------------------------------
HP is not enough: a body gets hurt in different ways, and it takes time to heal.

(A) Sprain [health.sprainHours]
  - Cause: a critical hit from a monster (25% chance), a fall, running with a heavy bag.
  - Effect: walk -30% speed, no running. Lasts 12 hours of the game.
  - Cure: Herbal Poultice (heals at once), or rest in a bed (halves the time).
  - Shown: the hero limps, a small bandage icon on the HUD.

(A) Cut and infection [health.infectionHours]
  - Cause: a hit from a monster with claws or blades (wolf, goblin, skeleton...), 15% chance.
  - A cut not cleaned in 6 hours becomes an infection: -1 HP per hour, max HP -20%, fever.
  - Fever: the screen is a little blurry at the edges, the hero sweats.
  - Cure: Clinic Antiseptic, or Aria at the clinic. A bath before the infection = no infection.

(A) Potion sickness [health.potionCount6h, health.potionCooldownHours]
  - More than 3 potions in 6 hours: nausea. The next potions heal 50% less, and the hero
    can vomit (-food).
  - The rule is written on the potion label, in English (see part 14): the player who reads
    it knows the rule. "Do not take more than three doses within six hours."
  - Cure: wait, or Honey-Lemon Elixir.

(B) Broken bone
  - Cause: a boss hit when HP < 20%, a fall from a cliff, a horse that throws the hero.
  - Effect: no weapon in the broken arm (magic only), or no running (broken leg). 3 days.
  - Cure: a splint (Aria), then rest. A healer with a high Sage class heals it in 1 day.

(B) Scars
  - A broken bone or a deep wound leaves a scar on the hero sprite (face, arm).
  - Some NPCs react: the guards respect it, the children ask about it, a romance says
    "you should be more careful".
  - Pure memory: no stat.

(B) Food poisoning and dysentery [health.dysenteryHours]
  - Cause: raw meat (already in the game: -10 HP), rotten food, untreated water (25% chance).
  - Dysentery: thirst goes down 2x faster, fatigue +50%. Lasts 1 day.
  - Cure: Honey-Lemon Elixir, clean water, rest.

(B) Sickness that spreads
  - In winter, a cold can pass from a villager to the hero (and back) when they talk.
  - A sick villager stays at home, the shop is closed ("Closed - the baker is ill"),
    the village asks for medicine: a side quest.
  - An epidemic (rare disaster, see society/disasters.js): many sick, the clinic is full,
    the price of herbs goes up.

(C) Heatstroke and hypothermia [health.heatstroke, health.hypothermia]
  - Desert at noon without water or a hood: heatstroke (-HP, blurred view, slower).
  - Snow at night, wet clothes, no fire: hypothermia (the cold of the game gets worse).
  - Clothes matter: a hood or a hat in the desert, a fur coat in the snow.
  - Waterproof Oil-Wax [health.waterproofHours]: the rain does not wet the hero for 48 hours.

(C) Cleanliness [health.cleanliness]
  - Goes down with fights (blood), mud, the swamp, the mines, days without a bath.
  - Low: shop prices +10%, romance -1 per talk, the guards look at you strangely, flies.
  - A bath (hot spring, home with Botanical Soap, a river in summer) brings it back.
  - Very low after a fight: higher risk of infection.

(C) Morale [health.morale]
  - Up: a good meal, a hot bath, a victory, a party with friends, music at the tavern,
    a gift received, sleeping in a real bed, a sunny day.
  - Down: a death in the village, a lost fight, rain for days, eating the same food,
    sleeping on the ground, being alone for long.
  - Low morale: XP -10%. High morale: +5% critical hits.
  - The Earth dishes (curry, omurice...) give the most morale: home sickness of the isekai.


5. SLEEP AND REST
-----------------
(A) Where you sleep changes the rest [health.wellRestedHours]
  - Own bed at home: 100% rest, "Well rested" for 8 hours (+10% XP, +1 time on questions).
  - Inn bed: 90%, costs Lexis. Tent (Waxed Field Tent): 80%. Ground: 50%, can be attacked.
  - Under the rain without a tent: 20%, and the cold gets worse.

(B) The night watch [health.watchMateId]
  - At a camp, a friend of the party can keep watch: no surprise attack at night.
  - Alone: 20% chance that a monster attacks during the night (the hero wakes up in a fight,
    with -25% time on the first question: they are still sleepy).

(B) Dreams
  - When the hero sleeps, sometimes a dream of their old life on Earth: a short text in
    English (a memory of an office, a train, an exam room). One RC question about it.
    Good answer = "Well rested" lasts longer. It is also part of the isekai story.


6. FOOD: IT ROTS, IT IS PRESERVED, IT IS VARIED
------------------------------------------------
(A) Food that rots [spoilage]
  - Each perishable food has a life in days (data/realism.js PERISHABLE_ITEMS):
    raw meat 3, raw fish 2, milk 2, egg 4, mushroom 4, bread 5, cooked dish 3.
  - The days left show on the item ("Fresh", "2 days", "Rotting"). Rotten = poisoning risk.
  - Rotten food is not lost: it becomes fertilizer for the fields (farm.js).

(A) Preserving
  - Salt (Crystal Salt) + raw meat = Smoked Jerky, never rots.
  - Drying Rack at home [workstations.dryingRack]: raw meat or fish dry in 1 day.
  - Ice-Chest at home [workstations.iceBox]: the food inside lasts 3x longer.
  - Pickling: vinegar + vegetables = Pickled Roots, lasts 30 days.
  - The home chest (home.js) keeps food cooler than the bag: x1.5.

(B) Varied food
  - The same food 3 times in a row feeds 30% less ("You are tired of carrots").
  - A day with meat + vegetables + bread: "Good diet", +5 max HP for the day.

(B) Food of the season
  - Spring: herbs, eggs. Summer: berries, fish. Autumn: mushrooms, apples, wheat.
    Winter: nothing grows outside, prices of food go up 30%, preserved food matters.
  - The farm follows the seasons: a carrot planted in winter does not grow (except in a
    greenhouse, built later).

(C) Cooking can fail
  - A burnt dish when the hero leaves the fire too long: food -50%.
  - A recipe is learned by reading it (a recipe card in English, see part 14).


7. EQUIPMENT: IT IS MADE, IT WEARS, IT IS CARED FOR
---------------------------------------------------
(A) Quality of the gear [combatRealism.quality]
  - Each weapon and armor has a quality: Worn x0.88, Standard x1, Fine x1.15,
    Masterwork x1.3 (data/realism.js QUALITY_TIERS).
  - The quality comes from the smith (Mira levels up with the village), the materials
    (ore, crystal, monster cores) and luck.
  - The name says it: "Fine Iron Sword".

(B) Rust and care
  - Rain and the sea make iron rust: wear 2x faster if not oiled.
  - Oil-Wax protects. Sharpening at a grindstone (home, forge) brings +5% damage for a day.

(B) Boots wear out [health.bootWear]
  - Each 1000 steps: -1% boots. Worn boots: the hero slips on ice, sprain more likely.

(C) Heirloom [combatRealism.heirloom]
  - A weapon used for 100 fights gets a name the player chooses, and +5% damage.
  - If it breaks, the smith can repair it but not replace it. Losing it is a sad event.


8. FIGHTS THAT FEEL REAL (but stay quick)
-----------------------------------------
The questions stay the heart of the fight. Realism changes the CONTEXT, not the questions.

(A) The place of the fight
  - Night without a torch: the monster sprite is dark, -10% time on questions.
  - Rain: fire magic -30%, lightning magic +30%.
  - Snow, mud, swamp: speed -20% (the time to answer depends on speed).
  - High ground (a hill, a bridge): +10% damage.

(A) Surprise [combatRealism.ambushReady]
  - Hit a monster from behind, or while it sleeps (at night, in its lair): a free first strike.
  - A monster that sees the hero first: it strikes first.
  - The Disguise Hood makes the hero harder to see.

(B) Monsters that act like animals
  - A monster with low HP can flee (it comes back later, hurt and angry).
  - Wolves hunt in packs at night. Slimes stay near water. Spiders wait in their webs.
  - A monster does not chase the hero forever: it goes back to its territory.
  - Monsters fight each other and eat the animals (see animals.js, animalFight.js).

(B) Formations [combatRealism.formation]
  - With friends in the party: Vanguard (+20% defense), Midguard, Rearguard
    (+22% magic, +15% time on questions). data/realism.js FORMATIONS.

(B) The nemesis [world.nemesis]
  - The monster that knocked out the hero gets a name ("Grak the Scarred"), a level up,
    and the hero's lost item. It comes back. The villagers talk about it.
  - Defeating it: a big reward, and the item back.

(B) Harvesting the monsters [skills.flayerXp, skills.flayerLevel]
  - After a fight, the hero can take parts: meat, pelt, bones, a monster core.
  - A clean kill (no wrong answer in the fight) gives better parts [skills.cleanKillStreak].
  - Skill levels up with use: more parts, better cores (coreShard, monsterCore, elderCore).

(C) Traps and locks [combatRealism.trappedFloor, combatRealism.pickedLocks]
  - Dungeons have hidden traps. A trap found = a short question (fast: under 8 s) to disarm.
  - A locked chest: Thief Lockpick + a question to open it. Wrong answer = the pick breaks.
  - Mimics: a chest can be a monster. The Appraisal Monocle shows it [combatRealism.mimicChecked].


9. A LIVING NATURE
------------------
(A) Resources run out [world.depletedSpots]
  - A tree cut, a rock mined, a bush picked: it comes back after days, not the next morning.
    Trees 7 days, rocks 5 days, bushes 3 days, fish in a spot 2 days.
  - Overharvesting a forest (cutting 20 trees in a zone): the forest shrinks for a season.

(B) Animal populations
  - Kill too many wolves: deer grow, they eat the crops of the farmers (a side quest).
  - Kill too many deer: the wolves get hungry and come near the village.
  - Each zone has a balance, shown in the Codex.

(B) Seasons change the world
  - Winter: snow on the ground everywhere in the north, rivers frozen (walk on them),
    the mountain pass closed by snow. Spring: rivers rise, some bridges are under water.
  - Autumn: leaves fall, mushrooms grow. Summer: the desert is deadly at noon.

(C) Lairs [world.lairDays]
  - Monsters live in lairs. A lair not cleared grows: more monsters each week.
  - A cleared lair stays empty for a while, then a new kind of monster moves in.


10. TIME AND THE ROUTINE OF THE WORLD
-------------------------------------
(A) Shops have hours
  - Open 8:00 - 19:00. The tavern 11:00 - 2:00. The clinic is always open (knock at night,
    Aria is grumpy: prices +20%).
  - The sign on the door says it, in English: "Opening hours: Mon-Sat 8 A.M. - 7 P.M."
  - A shop is closed on its rest day, and on festival days.

(A) Days of the week
  - The game counts a week of 7 days with names. Market day (once a week): more merchants,
    rare items. Temple day: the shrine blessing is stronger.

(B) People sleep
  - At night, the doors of the houses are locked. Entering = a crime (see part 12).
  - The guards change at 6:00 and 18:00: a moment when the gate is less watched.

(B) Travel takes time and effort
  - Walking on a road: normal speed. Off road: -15%. In the swamp or deep snow: -40%.
  - A long trip without rest: fatigue goes up faster.
  - Trail Cairns [world.trailCairns]: the player can build a small stone pile as a marker.
    It stays on the map.

(C) The calendar
  - Festivals at fixed dates (spring flowers, summer fishing, harvest, winter lights).
  - Birthdays of the NPCs: a gift that day counts double. Forgetting the birthday of a
    spouse: -friendship.


11. MONEY AND BUSINESS
----------------------
(A) The bank [bank.balance, bank.lastInterestDay]
  - A bank in each capital. Lexis in the bank are safe: when the hero is knocked out, they
    lose 10% of the Lexis they CARRY, not the bank Lexis.
  - Interest: 1% per week. Opening an account = reading the conditions (see part 14).
  - Bank Promissory Note: 500 Lexis that weigh nothing, cashed at any bank.

(A) Carrying money has a weight and a risk
  - 1000 Lexis = 1 kg in the bag.
  - Bandits attack more often a hero who carries a lot of Lexis on the road.

(B) Loans [debt.owed, debt.dueDay, debt.defaulted]
  - The bank lends money to buy a house or a big weapon. The contract is a text in English:
    the interest, the date to pay, the penalty. The player must read it (TOEIC part 7).
  - Late payment: a fine, then the bank takes an item, then the guards come.

(B) Taxes and rent
  - The house of the hero: a small tax each week to the village. The tax depends on the
    caste [social.caste] (data/realism.js CASTE_TIERS: a higher caste pays less).
  - The tax money really goes to the village treasury (economy/state.js), and it pays
    for the walls, the guards, the roads.

(B) Prices that move
  - Market booms [world.marketBoom] (data/realism.js MARKET_BOOM_DEFS): a war in the north =
    iron is expensive. A good harvest = wheat is cheap.
  - The price of an item is written on a price list in English at the market:
    a TOEIC-style table to read.

(C) Currencies of the kingdoms [currencies, fxRates]
  - Each kingdom has its coin (data/realism.js CURRENCIES). Lexis work everywhere, but a
    shop of another kingdom takes them with a 5% fee.
  - A money changer at each border. The exchange rates are on a board (a table to read).
  - Keep it light: ONE screen at the changer, not a wallet to manage every day.

(C) Investments and royalties [investments, patents.royalties]
  - Give Lexis to a workshop of a village: a share of its profit each week.
  - Hugo's inn pays royalties for the Earth recipes the hero teaches him (EARTH_ROYALTIES).
  - Merchant Stall Patent [patents.merchantLicense]: the hero can sell at the market himself.

(C) Counterfeit coins [counterfeitBatch]
  - Some merchants of bad districts pay in fake coins. The Appraisal Monocle sees them.
    Paying with a fake coin = a crime if a guard sees it.


12. LAW, CRIME AND REPUTATION
-----------------------------
(A) A crime needs a witness [codex.crimes]
  - Stealing, entering a house at night, hitting a villager: it is a crime only if someone
    sees it. A witness runs to the guards.
  - The guards then know the face of the hero for some days (a "Wanted" poster in English
    on the board, with a description: a text to read).

(A) Fines [fines.total, fines.bannedUntil]
  - A small crime: a fine. Not paid: a ban from the shops of the village for some days,
    then prison (kingdomLaws.js).

(B) Reputation travels
  - What the hero does in one village is known in the next one after 2 days (news carried
    by the merchants and the bards).
  - Good reputation: discounts, quests, people greet the hero by name.
  - Bad reputation: higher prices, closed doors, children hide.

(B) Titles and caste [social.caste, social.unlockedTitles, social.activeTitle]
  - From Vagabond to Realm Savior (CASTE_TIERS). The caste depends on the reputation, the
    guild rank and the wealth.
  - Titles are earned (Champion of the Arena, Slayer of Grak, Friend of Riverford...).
    NPCs use the active title when they talk to the hero.

(B) Factions [social.factions]
  - Merchant League, Arcane Order, Crown of Lexia (data/realism.js FACTIONS). Helping one
    can anger another. Each faction has its quests, its shop, its rewards.

(C) Tolls and permits [world.tollsPaidToday, world.permits]
  - Some bridges and roads have a toll. A permit (Crown Delve Charter) opens the dungeons
    without the salvage tax.

(C) Smuggling [smuggling.heat, smuggling.seizedValue]
  - Contraband already exists (kingdomLaws.js). Add the "heat": the more the hero smuggles,
    the more the guards search the bag at the border.


13. PEOPLE WHO REMEMBER
-----------------------
(A) Promises
  - When the hero accepts a quest, it is a promise. A quest not done in time: the NPC is
    disappointed, says it, and friendship goes down. A quest done: they tell others.

(A) NPCs remember the past
  - An NPC remembers the last gift, the last insult, the last time the hero saved them.
    They talk about it: "Thank you again for the flowers last week."
  - Each NPC keeps the 3 last important events with the hero.

(A) Death is permanent, and it is mourned [social.memorial]
  - A villager killed in a raid stays dead. Their house is empty, then a family member
    takes it, or it is sold.
  - A funeral the next day at the church: the villagers in black, a short text to read
    (a eulogy in English). The name goes on the memorial stone of the village.
  - Their family is sad for days: the shop of a dead merchant stays closed, their child
    does not play.
  - Inheritance: their items go to the family, sometimes a letter for the hero.

(B) Friends of the party have needs [social.companionMood]
  - They get hungry, tired, cold, like the hero. A hungry friend fights less well.
  - They complain ("Can we stop at an inn? My feet hurt."), they like and dislike places
    and choices. A friend who is badly treated (never fed, always hurt) leaves the party.
  - They have their own life: some days they want to visit their family.

(B) The children grow up
  - Children already grow from toddlers to youths (families.js). Then: at 40 days, they
    become adults with a job (often the job of a parent). The child of the hero can join
    the party.

(C) Jealousy and rivals [world.rivals]
  - Dating two people of the same village: they find out. Drama, a scene, maybe a breakup.
  - Rival adventurers take the same contracts at the guild. Sometimes they finish first.


14. ENGLISH IN THE WORLD (the most important part)
--------------------------------------------------
The TOEIC reading part (RC 55) is made of real life documents: notices, e-mails, memos,
invoices, ads, schedules, forms. The world of Lexia is full of them. Reading them well
changes what happens: this is realism AND training at the same time.
The texts come from the question bank (src/data/moreQuestions/: readingTexts, multiTexts,
rc8Texts), adapted to the world when needed, with their questions.

(A) The notice board of each village
  - Every morning, 1 to 3 new notices (a water cut, a festival, a missing child,
    a job, a road closed, a market day, a curfew).
  - Each notice has a question. Good answer = the hero understands, and the information
    matters: "The east bridge is closed on Tuesday" (it really is).
  - Wrong answer = the hero did not understand. They can read it again tomorrow.

(A) Labels and instructions
  - Potions: "Take one dose every six hours. Do not exceed three doses in a day."
    (= the potion sickness rule of part 4).
  - Seeds: "Plant in spring. Water daily. Harvest after five days."
  - Food: "Best before: day 12." (= the spoilage of part 6).
  - The player who reads learns the rules of the world by reading English.

(A) Contracts
  - Bank account, loan, rent of a house, a guild contract, a deal with a merchant.
  - A contract has the important terms in the text (price, date, penalty). Before signing:
    1 to 3 questions. A wrong answer = the hero signs a worse deal (higher interest,
    shorter delay) without knowing it. The real terms show later in the Journal.

(B) Letters and the post [social.courierSent]
  - The hero receives letters: from a friend, a family member of the village, the bank
    (a reminder), a lover, the Guild, the classmates (Lea, Hugo). An e-mail-like text and
    a question.
  - The hero can send letters (Courier Seal): to answer, the player picks the best reply
    among 4 (TOEIC part 6 style: the right sentence in the text).

(B) Schedules and timetables
  - The ferry of the Stormbreak Isles, the caravans, the stagecoach: a timetable on a board.
    The player reads it to know when to be at the port. Missing it = wait a day.

(B) Menus, price lists, invoices
  - The tavern menu (with prices, specials of the day: data/realism.js TAVERN_SPECIALS).
  - The smith gives an invoice for a repair. A mistake in the invoice is possible:
    the player who reads it carefully gets the money back.

(B) The news [world.news, world.lastNewsDay]
  - A town crier in the capitals, a bard in the taverns (data/realism.js BARD_RUMORS).
    The news tells what really happened in the world: the raids, the deaths, the deeds
    of the hero, the nemesis, the wars. A short article in English.

(C) Forms
  - Registering at the Guild, the bank, the border: a form to fill (choose the right
    answer for each field, like a TOEIC form question).

(C) Recipes and grimoires
  - A new recipe or spell is learned by reading it (data/realism.js GRIMOIRES).
    A short text and a question. Good answer = learned. The Scholar Reference Desk
    [workstations.studyShelf] gives +15% XP on these readings.


15. THE CODEX (what the hero has learned about the world) [codex]
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Monsters: seen, killed, their weaknesses (filled as the hero fights them).
- Recipes, discoveries, traps found, pearls found, baths taken, crimes.
- The map surveyed [world.surveyedPct, world.surveyRewardsClaimed]: rewards at 25/50/75/100%.
- A page per thing, with a short text in English.


16. WHERE THE HERO DIED [world.lastDeathPos]
-------------------------------------------
- When the hero is knocked out, part of the bag falls where they fell (not the equipment).
- A marker on the map. The hero can go back to pick it up, before a monster or a thief
  takes it (3 days).
- After a real death: a grave stays in the world in the next game (with the old name).

17. REALISM LEVEL (in the Options)
---------------------------------
Everyone does not want the same. One option, chosen at the start, changeable later:

  Light     Hunger, thirst, wounds heal alone, no spoilage, no potion sickness.
            For players who come for the English.
  Normal    Everything of part 2 marked (A) and (B).
  Hardcore  Everything. Wounds need care, food rots fast, money in the bag can be stolen,
            friends leave when badly treated. +20% XP to reward it.

The relaxed mode (+50% time to answer) stays separate: it is about English, not realism.

---------------

  r2  Wounds and potion sickness: sprain, cut, infection (part 4 A) + sleep places (part 5 A)

  r8  The realism option (part 17)
  r9  Everything marked (C)


20. WHAT NOT TO DO
------------------
- No mechanic that only adds a number to watch, with no choice for the player.
- No realism inside a question.
- No punishment the player could not see coming.
