LEXIA - SOUL
What the game needs to feel alive, to make the player care
==========================================================

This file adds to GAME_OBJECTIVE.txt, REALISM.txt and ISEKAI_TODO.txt.
The world of Lexia is already big and full of systems. What it lacks is not more mechanics:
it is moments, memories and people who want something.
All the names, scenes and lines below are first proposals, to change freely.
Priority: (A) first, (B) then, (C) later.


==========================================================
1. THE RULES OF SOUL
==========================================================

- A moment is worth more than a mechanic. One good scene beats ten new bars on the HUD.
- The world remembers. A big event (a death, a victory, a choice) changes what people say
  and what the world looks like, for a long time.
- People want things. Each important character has a wish, a fear and a secret.
- Losses are real. Somebody the player liked can be gone, and the world shows it.
- The player is known. The people use the hero's name, talk about what they did, and
  react to how they did it (fast, kind, cruel, absent).
- English first, still. Every scene is also something to read: a letter, a speech,
  a diary page, a notice. Soul and learning go together.


==========================================================
2. THE STORY BETWEEN THE BIG MOMENTS
==========================================================
Today there are 6 written scenes (story.js SCENES). After the death of Orin, nothing is told
until the gate of the Castle of Silence: chapters 7 to 10 are "go there, defeat the boss".

(A) A scene before and after each story boss
  - The Gerund Lich (Crystal Cave): a scholar who wanted to keep every word forever,
    and became a thing that only repeats them. A few lines before the fight, one after.
  - The Chrono Warden (Frostpeak Castle): the keeper of time who froze his castle on the
    day he lost someone. He asks the hero: "If you could stop one moment, which one?"
  - General Barbarism (Sandbandit Camp): "the teacher of war". The guardian ending says he
    taught Lyra. Show it in the hero path too: Lyra goes with the hero, they talk before
    the fight, and his last words are for her.
  - The 3 generals of the castle (Ravnos, Thamuz, Velmora) and Lilith, the Demon Queen:
    each one gets a voice, a reason to serve Solecis, and a last sentence.
    Velmora is "the Cursed General": who cursed her, and why?
  - Each boss scene is a short text in English (TOEIC style: a speech, a letter, a diary).

(A) A scene for each new chapter
  - Today a chapter ends with a toast ("Chapter done: ... +40 Lexis"). Replace it with
    a short scene: who gives the next task, why it matters, what they are afraid of.

(A) Solecis is present all along
  - The final boss should not appear for the first time at the end.
  - Through the Silence, Solecis speaks to the hero: in dreams, in the whispers of a raid,
    in a village that has lost its words. Short, sad lines, never the full truth.
  - The diary pages of Louis (data/isekai.js LOST_HERO_PAGES) each open a small scene:
    the hero reads the page aloud, and understands a bit more.

(B) Lexa, the goddess, comes back
  - Today she speaks only at the start. She should come back at the turning points:
    after Orin dies (she could not save him), before the castle (she is afraid too),
    after the end (a goodbye).

(B) A twist in the middle
  - Something the player believed is wrong. Proposals: Nyx was sent by the castle to
    watch the hero (and chose not to betray them); or Lexa is the one who summoned
    Louis, and feels guilty for what he became.


==========================================================
3. GRIEF AND MEMORY
==========================================================
Today, after Elder Orin dies, nothing in the code mentions him again
(only raid.js and chapters.js know his name).

(A) Orin is remembered
  - The next morning: a funeral on the square. The villagers come, Aria cries,
    Tomo does not speak. A eulogy to read (the church memorial exists in houses.js).
  - A grave in Eldermoor with his last words written on it: "Finish the sentence."
  - His house stays empty for a while, then a villager takes the role of elder.
  - In the final fight against Solecis, his words come back to the hero.

(A) Tomo, the child who saw it
  - For some days, Tomo does not talk. Then he asks the hero to teach him words, so that
    next time he can fight too. A small arc: he grows, he learns, he is proud of the hero.
  - If the hero ignores him, he becomes cold, then leaves to join the guard too young.

(A) The raids leave marks
  - The names of the villagers killed in a raid are carved on a memorial stone at the square.
  - Their family mourns them: a closed shop, a widow on the bench at night.
  - The burnt houses are rebuilt slowly, and the people talk about that night.

(A) Aria's three rescues feel different
  - Rescue 1: she is relieved and scolds the hero.
  - Rescue 2: she is afraid, her hands shake, she asks the hero to be careful.
  - Rescue 3: she is exhausted, she says it is the last time she can do it, and she means it.
  - After that, before each boss fight, she says something.

(B) The world remembers the big events
  - The memory of the NPCs exists (realism/socialAndCodex.js rememberNpcInteraction),
    but it has very few lines. Each big event should give a line to many people:
    the first raid, each boss defeated, each death, the wedding of the hero, a betrayal.
  - Gossip at the tavern and on the square changes with what happened this week.


==========================================================
4. PEOPLE WHO WANT THINGS
==========================================================
Today each NPC has 3 greetings ("hello") and 4 lines about their life ("life").
After a few talks, the player has heard everything.

(A) A personal arc for the main characters
  - 3 to 5 steps each, opened by friendship and by the chapters of the story.
  - Each arc has a wish, a fear and a secret. Proposals:
    * Aria: the Silence took her mother's voice. She wants to find out where the stolen
      voices go. Her arc ends in the Castle of Silence.
    * Mira: she wants to forge a blade that can cut the Silence. She needs rare
      materials, and she doubts herself.
    * Yuki: she keeps a forbidden book in the library. It is about Louis.
    * Garrick: he was on guard the night Orin died. He thinks it is his fault.
    * Nyx: she ran away from the castle. What did she see there? Who is she running from?
    * Lea (the rival classmate): she wants to be the one who ends the story. A duel
      that becomes respect, or a fall.
    * Hugo (the reborn classmate): he is happy here. He does not want to go back to Earth,
      and he is afraid the hero will.

(A) More lines, that change with time
  - Lines unlocked by friendship (stranger, friend, close friend, love).
  - Lines that depend on the chapter (before the first raid, after Orin, near the end).
  - Lines that depend on the season, the weather, a recent event.

(B) People talk about each other
  - The bonds exist in data/npcs.js (friend, rival, student, family). Turn them into
    scenes: Aria and Corvin argue, Mira teaches Darius, Elara and Aria share a tea.
  - The hero can make peace between two rivals, or take a side.

(B) People react to the hero
  - The way the hero plays is noticed: always fast ("You never hesitate."), often wrong
    ("Words are hard for you, eh?"), always away ("We never see you here anymore."),
    kind or cruel with villagers.


==========================================================
5. CHOICES WITH WEIGHT
==========================================================

(B) A few real dilemmas
  - A raid and a caravan are attacked the same night: the hero can only save one.
  - Barbarism is beaten: kill him, or let him go (Lyra asks for mercy).
  - The guards discover Nyx is a demon: hide her, or let them take her.
  - Lea asks the hero to let her strike the last blow against a general.

(B) Consequences that come later
  - A spared enemy comes back in the last chapter (to help, or to betray).
  - A person who was not saved is missing at the wedding, at the feast, in the epilogue.

(B) Endings that reflect what the hero did
  - Today the hero ending is 6 lines (+ the truth about Louis if the name is known).
  - Add an epilogue part for each important person: alive or dead, close or far,
    what became of them. ("Tomo became the youngest scribe of Eldermoor.")


==========================================================
6. SIDE QUESTS WITH STORIES
==========================================================
Today: 17 side quests, mostly "bring X" ("Firewood for the forge", "Branches for farm fences").

(B) Some quests that tell a story
  - Several steps, a twist, a choice. Proposals:
    * "A letter to the coast": the letter is a love letter. The hero reads it (a text to
      read) and discovers the sender is already dead.
    * "Monsters under the bed": the child is not afraid of monsters, but of the Silence
      that took his father's voice.
    * "Thieves of words": the thieves are children who sell stolen words to survive.
  - Some quests end sadly. Not every story has a happy end.

(C) Quests that come from the world
  - A quest born from what happened: after a raid, a family asks to find their lost dog;
    after the death of the baker, her son tries to bake the bread alone.


==========================================================
7. THREATS YOU CAN FEEL
==========================================================

(B) The Silence spreads, and you see it
  - The demon threat moves every day (war/demonThreat.js). Show it:
    a village taken by the Silence has blank signs, mute people, grey colors,
    no music. The player wants to give it back its voice.
  - Freeing a village = its colors, its music and its people's voices come back.

(B) Enemies with names
  - The nemesis of REALISM.txt (the monster that knocked out the hero gets a name and
    comes back) makes fear personal.
  - The emissaries and clans of the Demon King (ISEKAI_TODO.txt) each have a leader
    with a face, a voice and a reason.

(B) Before and after a raid
  - Before: the omen (it exists), then the village prepares: barricades, children hidden,
    a speech at the gate.
  - After: the morning after, the damage, the names, the hero walks among the people.


==========================================================
8. THE HERO'S OWN STORY
==========================================================

(B) A past on Earth
  - The hero was running to their TOEIC exam. Who were they? A job interview waiting,
    a parent who believed in them, a friend they never called back.
  - Dreams of Earth when the hero sleeps (REALISM.txt part 5): short texts in English,
    a memory each time.

(B) The final choice means something
  - The portal "Return to Earth & pass your TOEIC" exists (isekaiStructures.js).
    Make it a real goodbye: the hero says farewell to each friend, or decides to stay.
  - It is the meaning of the whole game: you learned the words, now you can go and use them.

(C) A small inner voice
  - A short thought of the hero at the big moments ("I couldn't save him.",
    "Is this what Louis felt?").


==========================================================
9. SMALL MOMENTS
==========================================================

(C) Campfire talks
  - The camp exists. Add short written talks between the friends of the party at night:
    their past, their jokes, their fears.

(C) Feasts, birthdays, weddings of the villagers
  - The hero is invited. A speech to read, a dance, a gift to choose.

(C) The children grow up
  - The babies of the village grow (families.js). As adults, they remember the hero:
    "You saved my mother during the third raid."

(C) Something to protect
  - A small animal that follows the hero home and waits at the door.


==========================================================
10. PRESENTATION
==========================================================

(B) Silence as an effect
  - The enemy is the Silence: use real silence. In the lands of the Demon King, the music
    fades, the footsteps get quiet. When a village is freed, the music comes back.

(B) A theme for the important characters
  - A short melody for Aria, Nyx, Solecis (audio/songs.js), played in their scenes.

(B) The faces in the dialogs
  - The expressions atlas exists (joy, sadness, anger, fear, surprise, love, disgust).
    Use them in every story scene: the face changes with the line.

(B) The monsters wear their grammar color
  - Today, the monsters of the sprite sheets are not tinted: a "gerunds" slime is blue.
    Give them the color of their category (a tint, an aura or a mark), like the README says.

(C) Scene direction
  - The camera moves slowly toward a speaker, a short pause before a big line,
    a fade to black after a death.


==========================================================
11. HOW TO KNOW IT WORKS
==========================================================

After 1 hour of play, ask a tester:
  - Can you name 3 characters? What does each one want?
  - Was there a moment you felt something (sad, angry, proud)?
  - Who do you want to protect? Who do you want to defeat, and why?
  - What do you want to do next in the game?
If the answers are vague, the game needs more soul, not more systems.
